> THE SITUATION
You are the wizard on the tower. Zombies shuffle toward you from the east. Your spellbook is the course material from weeks 1 through 3.

> HOW IT WORKS
• 20 questions, shuffled fresh every run.
• Every answer fires a fireball at the closest zombie.
• Right answer: +1 point, plus up to +5 for answering fast. The blast clears 2 zombies.
• Wrong answer: no points, the spell fizzles and only gets 1, and every zombie hops 2 seconds closer.
• 3 right in a row: the blast widens to 3. 6 in a row: 4.
• A zombie touches the tower and the run is over.
• The clock stops while you read the explanation. Take your time there.
• Waves get faster and thicker the longer you last.

> CONTROLS
Click an answer, or press 1 2 3 4. For true/false press T or F. Press ENTER to move on after the explanation.
